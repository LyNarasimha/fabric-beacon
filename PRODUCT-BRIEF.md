# Fabric Beacon — product brief

**Fabric Beacon** — Power BI Discovery & Governance Platform for workspaces, reports, and semantic models.

**Goal:** Build an internal discovery platform that lets the team search, browse, and understand every Power BI / Fabric asset in scope — with lineage, governance scores, and refresh health in one place.

**Description:** Centralized catalog for workspaces, datasets, reports, paginated reports, measures, columns, and DAX/M definitions. Deep search, reverse lookup, interactive lineage, and workspace governance.

**Success looks like:** In-scope workspaces synced; any asset found in seconds; clear lineage; governance and refresh visible; self-service documented for analysts and developers.

**What we can achieve:** One source of truth; faster impact analysis; proactive governance; fewer duplicates; foundation for trust and release readiness.

**What we can use it for:** Onboarding; impact before changes; pre-release workspace review; cleanup; migration; leadership estate view.
